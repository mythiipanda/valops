Method

Player Elo

Each player keeps one Elo across orgs and years.

Team strength is the mean of the current five. New lineups take a chemistry dock.

Every offseason moves each rating 30% toward the mean.

Match model

The model is logistic regression on team-strength diffs. Inputs include rating, form, schedule strength, map pool, and pistol skill. The fit uses 18 diffs in total.

Testing

We test walk-forward. The model trained through 2024 predicts 2025. The model trained through 2025 predicts 2026.

Train through 2024, test 2025: Brier 0.2203, accuracy 0.6429, n 504.

Train through 2025, test 2026: Brier 0.232, accuracy 0.631, n 588.

Coinflip Brier is 0.25.

A change ships only if walk-forward Brier improves.

Simulation

We run 10,000 sims over the real groups and bracket.

Player scores

SWING-AR is a player's total round swing minus replacement level, per 100 rounds.

Space counts first engagements per round.
