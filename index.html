How it works

The data

Every pro series, map by map and round by round: scores, kills, agents, economy. It lands in one database of teams, players, and maps, and every feature below is computed from history before match day. Nothing peeks at the future.

Player Elo
R′ = R + K(S − E)
E = 1 / (1 + 10(Rb − Ra)/400)

Ratings live on players, not teams, so they survive roster moves. Beating a stronger team moves a rating more. Every offseason, ratings drift 30% back toward average.

Two things changed in v4. First, credit for a series win is split by Round Swing, not kills: each kill is worth the change in round-win probability it caused, so a 1v3 clutch pays more than a cleanup kill. Second, ratings shrink toward average by sample size instead of a hard cutoff: a player with n maps counts as n/(n+20) themselves and 20/(n+20) average.

A team's rating is the mean of its five players, minus a penalty for a new lineup. The model runs two trackers side by side: a slow one for true strength and a fast one that reacts 1.5x to every result and never regresses. The Team strength table above is not a separate model: it just averages each team's pairwise win probabilities against the rest of the Champions field.

The features

Each matchup becomes a list of A-minus-B gaps: rating, recent form, last-60-day stats (rating, ACS, KAST, first-kill differential), series win rate, strength of schedule, days of rest, agent-composition coverage, pistol and retake rates. Small samples get smoothed toward average, so a three-map hot streak cannot break the math.

Win probability
P(A beats B) = 1 / (1 + e−w·x)

A plain logistic regression turns the gaps into a probability. The biggest weights sit on recent form, series win rate, and pistol rounds; features are correlated, so single weights are not all intuitive.

Simulation

We run 10,000 sims over the real groups and bracket.

Player scores
swing(kill) = P(win round | after) − P(win round | before)

Every kill is worth the round-win probability it added. A first blood in a 1v1 is worth far more than a trade in a 5v2, so stat padding dies here. The probabilities come from 2023-2024 rounds, keyed on attackers alive, defenders alive, and whether the spike is planted. Credit is zero-sum: what the killer gains, the victim loses. Plants and defuses move the state but earn no direct credit.

Round Swing = Σ (swing − agent average) / rounds × 100

Each player is measured against the prior-year average for their agent, so a duelist is judged against duelists. The result is per 100 rounds, then shrunk toward zero for small samples. The full-year board weights recent splits more heavily and counts international games more than regional ones, the same way the model trusts recent form and big stages; stage filters show each split raw. This replaced the old kill-share swing and the space metric.
